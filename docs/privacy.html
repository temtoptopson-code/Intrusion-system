Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes how the Network Intrusion Detection System (NIDS) prototype handles information when it is used.

Information that may be processed

  • Account details, such as name, email address, role and authentication-related data.
  • Network-event metadata submitted to or collected by an authorised NIDS sensor, which may include source and destination IP addresses, ports, protocol, timestamps, event type and byte counts. The optional sensor is designed to forward packet metadata, not decrypted packet contents.
  • Security alerts, investigation notes, IP watchlist/blacklist/whitelist entries, detection settings and administrative audit records.
  • Email addresses added by an administrator to receive security notifications.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the dashboard, analyse network-event metadata, display alerts and reports, manage accounts and settings, maintain audit history, and send configured security notifications.

Storage and sharing

Application data is stored in the database configured by the system operator. When email notifications are enabled, recipient addresses and alert message content are sent to the configured email delivery provider for delivery. Information may also be processed by the hosting and infrastructure providers used to operate the service.

NIDS is intended for use by the organisation or individual operating the deployment. The operator is responsible for deciding what networks and data are submitted, who is granted access, and how long records are retained. This prototype does not define a universal automatic retention period.

Security

Access is protected by application authentication and role-based permissions. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. Operators should use strong unique passwords, restrict access, protect credentials, keep services updated and configure secure transport and backups.

Your choices and requests

If you have questions or want to request access, correction or deletion of information held by a particular NIDS deployment, contact the administrator who operates that deployment. The administrator controls the database and can determine whether a request can be fulfilled.

Children

This prototype is not designed for children and is intended for authorised organisational or academic use.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the project changes. For questions about this policy or the project, contact temtoptopson@gmail.com.
